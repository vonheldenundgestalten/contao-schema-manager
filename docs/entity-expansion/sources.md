## Method and limits

Research date: **5 October 2026**. Inspected **52 public URL responses** across the six projects and their related domains, including representative detail pages, organizational information, cases, events/training and existing JSON-LD. This is a targeted architecture study, not an exhaustive crawl or a certification/legal audit.

Read public page text and embedded JSON-LD, then compared findings with the released 1.2.0 extension code and the parked calendar branch. No client records were modified, no forms submitted, and no paid AI analysis run. Public HTML cannot establish database schemas, full legal ownership or permission to publish private records. User-supplied facts about source tables are labelled as such in the findings.

The table lists types found recursively in **raw JSON-LD scripts**, including nested types. It is not a full structured-data validation. No scripts detected does not rule out microdata, RDFa, JavaScript-generated output or another response variant. Relative types/IDs are not normalized here. A successful HTTP response can still redirect to an unrelated homepage; redirects are called out. Download links were inspected, but certificate PDFs were not independently authenticated or exhaustively reviewed.

Local raw research captures stay in ignored `.deploy/entity-research/`; this documentation preserves the relevant observations and public sources. Content can change after this date. Do not treat inferred mappings as confirmed facts.
